Figure 2 Overview of CalibAll. It follows a coarse-to-fine calibration pipeline that achieves training-free, stable, andaccurate camera extrinsic estimation across diverse datasets and robot platforms. CalibAll first use EEF Recognitionto obtain the end-effector tracking point, then apply temporal PnP to estimate a coarse extrinsic, and finally performextrinsic refinement to obtain an accurate result.

Figure 1 Current action representations suffer from inconsistent semantics across robot datasets: joint actions varyin dimensionality, end-effector poses depend on robot-specific base frames and EEF definitions, and delta actions useinconsistent local frames. CalibAll resolves these inconsistencies by estimating camera extrinsics for offline datasets andconverting heterogeneous actions into standardized TCP poses in the camera frame. We use this pipeline to build aCamera-Frame Action Dataset for cross-embodiment pretraining, leading to state-of-the-art downstream performance.

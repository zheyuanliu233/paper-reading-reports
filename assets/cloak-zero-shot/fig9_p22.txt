Figure 9: Ablation rollouts on the unseen Sharpa hand for the move task, prompt “Move the cubenext to the white mug”. We compare Cloak-VLA against two ablations that share its architecturebut change the training mask: Cloak-VLA-NoAug, trained on the bare source-embodiment maskwith no augmentation, and Cloak-VLA-Overlap, a Shadow-inspired [26] variant that overlays thesource mask with a known target silhouette. Both ablations either omit augmentation or assumethe target embodiment is known in advance, isolating mask augmentation as the component thatcloses the embodiment gap. Only Cloak-VLA grasps the cube and completes the move, while bothablations fail to secure it, highlighting that mask augmentation is necessary for a robust wrist-viewrepresentation on an unseen embodiment.
